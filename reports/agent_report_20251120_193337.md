*   **Contact Information:**

    *   **General Phone:** 311 (within Evanston) or 847-448-4311
    *   **Planning & Zoning Division:** 847-448-4311
    *   **Building & Development Services:** 847-448-4311
    *   **Building Permits and Inspections:** 847-448-8207, option 5 to schedule an inspection
    *   **Zoning Administrator:** Melissa Klotz, 847-448-4311
    *   **Staff Contact, Zoning Committee:** Meagan Jones, 847-448-4311, mmjones@cityofevanston.org
    *   **Email:** Use the general contact form on the City of Evanston website.
    *   **Website:** [https://www.cityofevanston.org/](https://www.cityofevanston.org/)

*   **Service Areas:** The City of Evanston is divided into zoning districts that determine land use activities and building types. Use the "About My Place" tool on the City of Evanston website to find a property's zoning district.

*   **Step-by-Step Connection/Application Requirements (General Process for Building Permits):**
    1.  **Application:** Submit a building permit application through the Citizen Portal.
    2.  **Zoning Analysis (if needed):** If the project includes exterior work, a Zoning Analysis Application may be required.
    3.  **Plan Review:** Building and Zoning staff review the project. Additional reviews may be required from Engineering, Preservation, and/or Fire departments.
    4.  **Permit Issuance:** Once approved, Permit Technicians review the application documents.
    5.  **Building Inspections:** City staff will conduct inspections as construction proceeds.
    6.  **Certificates of Occupancy:** After all final inspections are approved, submit a Certificate of Occupancy Request Form - Residential for review.

*   **Required Documents and Fees:**
    *   **Required Documents (General):** Building permit application (online via Citizen Portal), electronic copies of plans, current Plat of Survey (for exterior work/additions), Zoning Analysis Application (if required), total estimated construction cost, contractor information.
    *   **Fees:** Refer to the Permit Fee Schedule on the City of Evanston website. Re-inspection fee: $70 for same-day cancellations, missed, or failed inspections. Surface parking lot fees will increase to $110 starting February 1, 2025.

*   **Technical Specifications:** The City of Evanston has adopted several International Codes with local amendments, including the 2021 International Building Code (IBC), 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), 2020 NFPA 70 National Electric Code (NEC), and others. The Illinois Stretch Energy Code is effective October 1, 2025.

*   **Important Policies and Regulations:** Zoning Ordinance (Title 6 of the Evanston City Code), Planned Developments, Inclusionary Housing Ordinance, Short-Term Residential Rentals, Landscaping requirements.

    *   **Application Process:** Online via ComEd website or by phone.
    *   **Required Information:** Service address, mailing address, Social Security Number, two forms of identification (one government-issued photo ID), contact information of property owner (if applicable), proof of payment of past due ComEd debts (if applicable).
    *   **Deposits:** Determined by Illinois Commerce Commission rules.
    *   **Additional Information:** May be required in certain circumstances.

*   **Important Policies and Regulations:**
    *   **Community Choice Aggregation (CCA):** Evanston participates in a CCA program; MC-Squared Energy Services, LLC is the current approved provider for the supply charge.

    *   **Financial Assistance:** Various programs for income-eligible customers, veterans, and non-profits.
    *   **Energy Choice:** Residents can choose alternative electricity suppliers, but ComEd remains the default utility for delivery.
    *   **Municipal Inspections:** ComEd requires confirmation of municipal inspection for new permanent electric service.
